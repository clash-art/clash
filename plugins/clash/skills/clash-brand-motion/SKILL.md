# Brand motion

Produce the requested title, sting, character beat, or motion system. Use the
base `clash` skill for product execution and `clash-mg-character` for authored
Remotion characters when applicable. Do not require a mascot for a typography task.

## Identity and motion intent

Inspect supplied wordmarks, artwork, fonts, palette, and proportions. Keep exact
brand geometry and text through an appropriate authored composition workflow;
generated approximations of the logo are not equivalent. Establish the message
and the intended character of movement: precise, playful, calm, energetic, or
another concrete direction from the brief.

Translate that direction into spatial and temporal decisions. Define the focal
element, entrance, transformation, emphasis, and final reading state. Use one
coherent motion motif rather than unrelated effects on every element.

```text
Animate [supplied artwork/title/original character] for [communication purpose].
Preserve [exact geometry, typography, palette, and proportions].
Begin at [readable initial state].
Move through [anticipation, main action, response, and settle].
Emphasize [event] with [timing, spacing, weight, or contrast].
Resolve into [final composition] with enough time to read [message].
```

## Author and refine

Use editable source when requested. Assign deliberate timing and easing to
related movements; keep contacts, pivots, and weight plausible for characters.
Ensure masks and transitions reveal the intended element without accidental
clipping. Typography should remain readable through and after its transition.

For a loop, inspect the boundary and derivative of movement rather than matching
only the first and last still. For a sting, let the end state settle and hold.
Use audio only when requested or supplied, and inspect synchronization in the
actual render. Do not treat silent frame samples as sound validation.

## Acceptance

Render through the actual Project path, inspect full-speed motion and selected
frames, and verify identity, legibility, contact, timing, and final state. Keep
editable source and selected output identities. Repair the specific animation
cause instead of hiding defects with a global fade or camera shake.
