# Review scenes and deliver the film

The atomic skills own their acceptance criteria: compare views with
`clash-multiview-consistency`, inspect composed inputs with
`clash-reference-composition`, and review motion/sound across cuts with
`clash-shot-continuity`. Load the one relevant to the defect instead of
repeating a whole production workflow.

## Decide whether the scene works

Watch the sequence at normal speed with sound. Beyond technical agreement,
check whether the scene's intention and change read, whether reactions belong
to the same exchange, and whether the pacing gives the important action time
to register. A technically matching clip can still be the wrong performance.

Record the concrete defect and the affected selection. Change its input,
staging, performance, or edit decision before another batch. Preserve
independent accepted work; do not propagate an unreviewed replacement design.
An unavailable inspection capability leaves that dimension unverified.

## Preserve revisions and select replacements

Keep selected references and takes identifiable by their actual Asset and
source Revision identities. A changed costume, set, performance, or reference
selection needs an identifiable version. Find affected shots and explicitly
choose replacements while preserving the earlier cut and its inputs.
Rejected or pending outputs must not silently enter the final Timeline.

## Verify the delivered film

Save the real editable Timeline. Use the Timeline and finishing skills to
check total timing, picture, sound, captions, and referenced media. Decode
and review the rendered deliverable before claiming completion.

State the evidence precisely: a reviewed reference, sampled motion, a
continuous scene, an editable project, and full playback establish different
things. A shot-list duration sum proves planned length; a submitted render
proves that a job was accepted. Neither proves a finished playable film.
