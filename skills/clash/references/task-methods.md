# Choose a creative method

When the task provides a skill pack, use its mounted skill paths and load only
what the requested output needs. Category workflows are `clash-product-ad`,
`clash-talking-head`, `clash-explainer`, `clash-narrative`, `clash-music-video`,
and `clash-brand-motion`; they cover advertising, speech editing, explanation,
narrative, music, and brand motion respectively. A category does not require
an unrequested medium or a full production. For a specific creative operation,
load only its atomic skill:
`clash-multiview-consistency` for views of one subject (people, products, props,
creatures, vehicles, or environments), `clash-reference-composition` for an image
assembled from selected references, or `clash-shot-continuity` for adjacent video
shots. For a narrative film, `clash-film-production` coordinates those skills
with scene rehearsal and delivery. For cover artwork, information graphics,
or sequential comics, use `clash-cover-image`, `clash-infographic`, or
`clash-comic` respectively; these adapted scene workflows reuse the atomic
skills and preserve their upstream attribution. Use the Director skill for actual Stage blocking/capture,
and the Timeline, motion-graphics, or finishing skill for their respective
creative work. This skill owns transport navigation and product evidence,
not the artistic recipe.
